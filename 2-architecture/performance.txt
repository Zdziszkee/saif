HOW FAST IT IS (REAL MEASUREMENTS)
===================================

We timed the real pipeline on a laptop (Bun, 60 runs each):

- Known secret, blocked by patterns: ~0.4 ms average. The AI judge
  was never called.
- Known jailbreak, blocked by threat intel: ~0.3 ms. Judge skipped.
- Harmless message, full pipeline: ~0.2 ms of pipeline work, plus one
  AI-judge call: ~51 ms over local network (a real hosted judge takes
  hundreds of milliseconds to low seconds).

The takeaway: everything except the AI call takes less than a
millisecond. So the design avoids that call whenever the cheap passes
already have an answer — blocked junk costs ~0.3 ms and never touches
the network, and normal traffic pays exactly one judge round trip.

Reproduce it yourself: `bun run test:integration:mock`.
