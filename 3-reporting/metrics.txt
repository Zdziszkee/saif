WHAT GETS MEASURED
==================

Every number on the dashboard comes from the audit log — one record
per decision — so the screen can never disagree with what actually
happened.

- Verdict counts: allowed, scrubbed, blocked, sent-for-review —
  overall and per user.
- Who caught it: credit per guardrail (patterns, threat intel,
  AI judge) and per attack kind.
- Scrub volume: how many sensitive spans were replaced, and how long
  each decision took.
- Review queue: every flagged item with the evidence a human needs.
- System health: feed version, active policy, judge on/off and why.
- Spending: tokens per user and model, dollar cost where known
  (unknown models show "unknown", never zero), plus budget caps.
- Export: the whole trail downloadable for offline analysis.
