WHERE THE CODE LIVES (READ IN THIS ORDER)
============================================

The entire enforcement path is about six small files:
- pipeline.ts — the stage order, when the AI call gets skipped, how
  scrubbed text flows, and what happens when something errors.
- apply.ts — how evidence turns into a verdict, and how strictness
  dials change the answer.
- The three inspectors: pattern detectors, threat-intel matching,
  and the AI-judge client with its question catalog.
- gateway.ts — the model proxy: who called, over budget?, inspect,
  forward-or-refuse, meter the usage.

The rules are data, not code: policy.json (strictness, rules,
approved models, budgets), policy.jev.json (the judge's questions),
policy.mcp.json (tool permissions), signatures.json (generated threat
feed). Doubles for keyless demos live in scripts/mock-*.ts; tests
fill the tests/ folder (unit, matrix, showcase, and live tiers).
