THE GUARDRAILS, PLAINLY STATED
==============================

Three inspection passes run in order. The strictest result wins.

- Pattern detectors find emails, secrets, credentials, and ID numbers,
  and replace them with placeholders like [EMAIL]. They also catch
  encoded or scrambled payloads trying to sneak past.
- A threat-intel feed (built from MITRE ATLAS and OWASP snapshots plus
  our own curated entries) recognizes known attacks: injections,
  jailbreaks, fake system messages, credential-file grabs, shell pipes
  hidden in tool calls. Each pattern carries its own severity and
  action, overridable one by one.
- An AI judge answers simple yes/no policy questions about the text
  (is this an injection? insider trading? ...) with a probability each.
  Anything it's unsure about gets marked uncertain instead of guessed.

Who gets what:
- Every caller identifies itself, and each team gets its own strictness
  setting. The same evidence can warn one team and block another.
- Only approved models and endpoints can be used; spending caps cut off
  runaway usage; tool calls go through per-team allowlists, with a
  human confirm step for the dangerous ones.

Every door uses the same pipeline — the chat API, the playground, tool
calls, and the model gateway — so there's no side entrance. Each
decision is logged with who, what, why, and how sure, and anything
flagged for review lands in a queue for a human.
